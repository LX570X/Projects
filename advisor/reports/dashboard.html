Context & catalysts

  • Warsh - the event resolved The keynote, 'In Our Time', was hawkish in substance and guidance-free in form. Verbatim: this summer's better PCE and CPI readings 'do not tell me that underlying trends have meaningfully improved'; otherwise 'we have work to do'. He retired forward guidance outright - it 'has overstayed its welcome' - and called the balance sheet 'trillions larger than it needs to be'. He did NOT mention Bessent's buybacks or term premium, which reads as independence rather than accommodation. September hike odds repriced from about one-in-three to a coin flip (CME ~54-59%, Kalshi 48%). But the curve BEAR-FLATTENED - 2-year +6bp to 4.298%, 30-year MINUS 2bp to 5.168% - so the long end rallied on a hawkish speech, pricing Fed credibility rather than fiscal dominance, which de-risks the Sept 9 buyback doubling as a disorder catalyst. Equities barely moved (S&P -0.25%, still a winning week); the dollar rose 0.41% and gold fell about 2.5%, with crypto falling almost exactly in line with gold - a shared real-rate shock, not a crypto-specific one. Verification limits: the Fed site was unreachable, so all quotes are press-corroborated across outlets rather than read from the transcript; the circulating '2% is unwavering and non-negotiable' line is uncorroborated and is not attributed to him here; and he appears to have said nothing on stablecoins or the symposium's own payments theme.
  • Positions The pre-commitment was honoured in full and nothing triggered. ETH 2,434.17, $88.60, +33.0%; stop 2,105 is 13.5% below spot and the 2,300 close-exit 5.8% below with no count begun. BTC 77,441.66, stop 73,289, 5.4% below and never approached (intraday low ~76,877). LINK 11.334, stop 9.80, 13.5% below. All three 3xATR candidates negative. LESSON: the published scenario map put a hawkish outcome at ~$73,000 BTC, essentially our stop. The content WAS hawkish and BTC fell 2.8% - the mapping was wrong because August's rally came from the Treasury buyback rather than Fed easing hopes, so a hawkish Fed had little of the right kind of length to liquidate. Similarly-constructed scenarios for Sept 15-16 will be discounted.
  • Crypto structure - cleaner at a lower price Three things improved through the event. RSI is back in the 55-75 band across the complex for the first time in over a week, and scores now EQUAL core scores again, so last week's stretch distortion is out of the rankings entirely. Funding fell to about 0.0024% per 8h (~2.6% annualised) from 8-15% the prior week, so the $488m of liquidations was macro repricing rather than a leveraged unwind. And the overbought condition resolved through time and a shallow decline rather than a break. Independent corroboration worth knowing without acting on: published ETH work puts the structural break at 2,300, identical to our two-close trigger, and published BTC work treats the whole 70,000-90,000 band as positioning noise, so the 73,289 stop sits inside noise rather than at a break.
  • UAE - Monday absorbs four days at once On Aug 28, while DFM and ADX were shut, Treasury moved to revoke Banque Misr's UAE branch access to US financial institutions over Iran ties - roughly $1.8bn processed over two years for about 100 companies tied to Iran's shadow banking network - and OFAC designated the regional manager of Bank Melli Dubai. The distinction that matters: no UAE-LISTED bank has been named in any tranche; Banque Misr is Egyptian state-owned and Bank Melli is Iranian, so this is sector sentiment risk rather than index or constituent risk. Cutting the other way, the UAE's own suspension of Iran trade ties lowers the odds a listed entity appears later, and ADX has been reacting more than DFM - du is DFM-listed. Monday stacks a four-day-gap reopen, the MSCI implementation close (du in; Taaleem and Deyaar out of small-cap), and the resulting volume into one flow-driven session. Bessent promised more detail during the week, so this is live.
  • ADA - a dated idiosyncratic risk ADA fell 4.39%, more than any name in the feed, and lost STRONG BUY on a MACD bearish cross. The cause is dated and specific: Cardano's constitutional-committee renewal expires September 1. Four of seven seats lapse at epoch 653 and the renewal needs 67% DRep support against 51.68% currently, with SPO support at roughly 8-15% against a 51% bar - failing, not close. Failure drops the committee below its five-member minimum, freezing hard forks, parameter changes, constitutional amendments and treasury spending. No position held, and this is a reason to keep it that way.

Method & risk
